## Key source excerpts

> “Every Company Admin has an agent that can: prepare and summarize; monitor state and service commitments; draft tasks, notices, offers, reports, and reconciliations; retrieve relevant corpus and policy material; detect missing evidence or stalled work; recommend assignments and escalation; and explain its basis.” — [S2]

> “The agent does not independently bind the company, move money, grant authority, make coverage decisions, practice law, perform licensed adjusting, accept an offer, or change production permissions unless a narrowly defined, lawful, tested delegation explicitly permits it.” — [S2]

> “Use roles based on responsibilities, not mystical agent counts.” — [S3]

> “Likely first agents: Documentation Agent, Product Agent, Data Model Agent, Backlog Agent, QA Agent, Legal Review Agent, Sponsor Package Agent, Kimosabe Coordinator Agent.” The source marks the question **OPEN**. — [S4]

> “Create Company Admin agents in read/draft/recommend mode.” — [S7]

## What the source establishes

The Human Blockchain material establishes a responsibility-led cross-functional team, a bounded Company Agent supporting each Company Admin, a clean-clone One Prompt Event, a work lifecycle that includes independent review and human gates, and a strong prohibition against agents substituting for legal or binding human authority. It also establishes that knowledge must be source-grounded, uncertainty preserved, and Git used for shared continuity/review.

## What the source does not yet establish

The source does **not** settle the exact first team topology; agent count; runtime; model/provider policy; tool permissions; data-access classes; memory partitioning; routing logic; concurrency policy; escalation SLA; cost/usage guardrails; schedule; deployment configuration; or promotion thresholds. The direct open status of Q212 is decisive: do not portray the proposed team design as a source-approved configuration.
